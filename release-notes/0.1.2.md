**Fixed**
- Words were split wherever pdf.js emits them in pieces (around a ligature or a font change: "work fl ow", "(TNF α )"). Text items whose boxes touch are now joined; a space is inserted only across a real gap or a column jump.
- Words broken at a line end stayed broken ("indepen- dently"). They are rejoined when the document spells the word unbroken elsewhere or when the halves are not words on their own; genuine compounds (high-level, trapped-ion) keep their hyphen. Over 12 library PDFs: 821 of 928 line-end splits joined, every kept hyphen a real compound.

Together these make the quotes *Find quotes across papers* and *Ask this paper* return match the PDF text verbatim: in the live check over three real papers, 10 of 14 quotes were verbatim with the right page before the fix and 12 of 14 after; the two left are a spelling the document never attests ("long-standing") and a footnote that pdf.js interleaves mid-sentence.
